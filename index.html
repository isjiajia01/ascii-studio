    Questions, answered.

    What is ASCII art?

    ASCII art draws a picture using only text characters. Dark areas get dense characters such as @ and #, light areas get thin ones such as . and :, so from a distance the letters add up to the image. This generator also makes braille, halftone dots, dither and brick mosaics.

    How do I turn a photo into ASCII art?

    Open the generator and drop in a photo, or paste one, and it converts at once. Pick a style or a preset, use Detail to set how many characters wide it is, then save it as an image.

    Can I turn a video into ASCII art?

    Yes. Add a video and it converts live as it plays. Export saves an MP4 at 720p, 1080p or 4K, up to a minute long. Every frame is converted, so the result is smooth even on a slow machine. MP4 export needs a browser with WebCodecs (Chrome, Edge, Safari 16.4+).

    Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

    No. The conversion runs in your browser on your own device, so your pictures and videos never leave it.

    What is the difference between ASCII, braille and halftone?

    ASCII styles pick a letter or symbol by brightness. Braille uses tiny dot patterns, so each cell holds up to eight dots and the detail is finer. Halftone (Round dots) draws dots of different sizes, like a newspaper print.

    What is dithering, and when should I use it?

    Dithering fakes shades with patterns of dots in only two tones, the look of old one-bit screens. It suits logos, flat graphics and posters. For photos with soft shading, Braille or Detailed usually looks smoother.

    How do I make the Matrix digital rain effect?

    Open the generator and click Digital rain in the preset strip. It gives you green 1s and 0s on black, with a glow and a shimmer, and it moves when you add a video or turn Animate on.
